# Dementia Helper

**Dementia Helper** is a Flutter-based medication reminder and tracking app designed to support people managing daily medications and the caregivers who assist them.

The app provides a simple interface for organizing medication schedules, receiving reminders, and monitoring medication adherence.

## Features

- **Medication Reminders:** Schedule daily local notifications for medications.
- **Patient Mode:** Add, edit, delete, and mark medications as taken.
- **Caregiver Mode:** View medication schedules, daily intake status, and remaining pill quantities.
- **Pill Tracking:** Monitor remaining medication quantities with low-stock indicators.
- **Privacy-Focused:** Medication information is stored locally on the device, without accounts or cloud storage.

## Privacy

Dementia Helper does not collect personal information or use advertising, analytics, or external databases.

[Privacy Policy](https://marilenatsiopoulou.github.io/dementia_helper/privacy.html)

## Disclaimer

Dementia Helper is an organizational and reminder tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and is not a substitute for professional healthcare guidance.
