Dementia Helper Support

Thank you for using Dementia Helper.

Dementia Helper is a medication reminder and tracking application designed to help users organize daily medications and provide caregivers with a clear view of medication status.

Using Dementia Helper

With Dementia Helper, users can:

  • Add medications and scheduled reminder times
  • Receive local medication reminders
  • Record when a medication has been taken
  • Track the number of pills remaining
  • Edit or delete medications
  • View medication status in Patient and Caregiver modes

Notifications

Dementia Helper uses local notifications to provide medication reminders.

If reminders are not appearing, check that notifications are enabled for Dementia Helper in your device settings.

Privacy

Medication information is stored locally on the user's device. Dementia Helper does not use advertising or analytics services and does not transmit medication information to the developer.

View the Dementia Helper Privacy Policy

Medical Disclaimer

Dementia Helper is an organizational and reminder tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and is not intended to replace guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.
